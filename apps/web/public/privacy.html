Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Sahay ("we", "our", or "us") is a privacy-conscious mutual-aid matchmaker that connects people at the same event who need an essential item with neighbours already carrying it. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we collect your name and email address (or phone number if you choose SMS sign-in).
  • Event and request data. We store the events you join, items you request or offer, and messages exchanged with matched neighbours within the app.
  • Device & usage data. We collect standard crash reports and anonymous usage analytics (session length, screen views) to improve reliability. This data cannot be linked back to you.
  • Push notification tokens. If you grant permission, we store a device token solely to deliver in-app notifications; we do not use it for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match you with neighbours who can help or be helped at your event.
  • To send notifications about match status and messages (only if you opt in).
  • To investigate abuse reports and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve app performance.

We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We share personal data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Matched users. When a match is made, your first name is shown to the other person so you can identify each other. No other personal details are shared automatically.
  • Service providers. We use infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, push notification services) who process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

4. Data Retention

Active account data is retained while your account is open. Closed event data (requests, offers, messages) is automatically purged within 30 days of the event ending. You may delete your account at any time from the app's Settings screen, which permanently removes your personal information within 14 days.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over TLS. Passwords are never stored — we use email magic links or provider OAuth exclusively. We apply the principle of least privilege so internal systems access only the data they need.

6. Children's Privacy

Sahay is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights please contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of Sahay after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
